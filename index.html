Where's Expert mode?
On tmsearch.uspto.gov, look for the Basic / Expert toggle near the search box (top of the results/search panel). Switch to Expert, then in the search-by dropdown choose "Field tag and search builder" specifically — other options (Wordmark, Owner, etc.) will reject this query's CM:/IC:/LD: syntax entirely.
