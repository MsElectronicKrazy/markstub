Where's Expert mode?
On tmsearch.uspto.gov, look for the Basic / Expert toggle near the search box (top of the results/search panel). Switch to Expert, then in the search-by dropdown choose "Field tag and search builder" specifically — other options (Wordmark, Owner, etc.) will reject this query's CM:/IC:/LD: syntax entirely. Paste the query above into the search bar and run it. Sound-alike — same pronunciation, different spelling
Homophone and common name-spelling substitutions (Michelle/Michele, Daniel/Danielle) worth a quick look — spelling alone can change how many live marks come back.
Partial matches — one word at a time
Marks that contain just one of your words. Expect a lot of noise — a common first name or surname will match many unrelated marks, so keep your class selected and read only the ones where that word is the whole mark or its dominant part.
Stylized — common startup-naming substitutions (k/c, z/s, dropped vowels)
